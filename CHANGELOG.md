# Changelog

What changed in each release, in plain words. `scripts/release.sh` puts a version's section in the
update window and on the GitHub release, and stops when the section is missing.

## 1.0.0

The first release of Clickity.

- Record a screen, a window or an area, with system audio, microphone and camera.
- Clicks zoom in by themselves, and a smooth Clickity cursor is drawn over the recording.
- Trim, cut, speed up, blur private areas and put the video on a styled background.
- Export MP4, ProRes or GIF.
- Screenshots with a quick card to copy, save, annotate or pin.
- Open at Login, in Settings → General.
